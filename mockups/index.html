App mockups · v0.1 · for discussion

Twelve screens.
Ten seconds a day.

A first pass at the whole product: onboarding, the daily loop, the day-14 payoff, and the social layer. Every screen has one job and one obvious tap.

Rule 01

Never in the way.

No blocking, no timers before an app opens. If it interrupts posting a photo, it gets deleted.

Rule 02

One tap, no typing.

Check-ins are a face, a number, or a game. Two prompts a day at most.

Rule 03

Show the cause.

Not "you used Instagram for 2h". Instead: what you did, and how the next day felt.

Rule 04

Yours only.

Usage, never content. On the phone. Friends see game scores, nothing else.

System

Black, white, and one signal colour. The orange only ever marks the thing that matters on a screen.
